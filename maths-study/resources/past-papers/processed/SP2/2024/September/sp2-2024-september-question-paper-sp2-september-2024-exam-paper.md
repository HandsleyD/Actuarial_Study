6     A life insurance company sells term assurance business via direct sales and via the
      internet.

      The company is concerned that their term assurance premiums appear uncompetitive
      in price tables published both in the media and its own marketing material. These
      tables display premiums from various companies at pre-selected ages and terms,
      under the heading ‘Best Term Assurance Deals’ (e.g. top five term assurance
      premium rates available for a male aged 45, term 25 years).

      The management of the life insurance company has proposed substantially reducing
      the premiums for the ages and terms included in the table to make the company
      appear either cheapest or second cheapest at each pre-selected age and term.

      This will be balanced by increasing premiums at other ages and terms so that the
      company’s overall profitability based on their existing business mix would be
      unchanged.

      Comment on the advantages and disadvantages of this proposal.                         [8]

8     A life insurance company has a range of with-profits products on sale and regular
      reversionary bonuses are currently awarded on a compound basis.

      The company is considering switching to award regular reversionary bonuses on a
      super-compound basis for new policies.

      (i)     Explain why the company may want to move to a super-compound basis.           [3]

      The company currently offers a compound bonus rate of 4% p.a.

      The proposed new basis will add bonuses on a super-compound basis at 3% p.a. on
      the basic sum assured and 7% p.a. on attaching bonuses.

      The target total return on the product is 7% p.a. of the original sum assured. At
      maturity, the company plans to award a terminal bonus to achieve this overall target
      return. The terminal bonus rate is calculated as a percentage of the attaching regular
      reversionary bonuses required to achieve this aim.

      As part of the company’s review of the newly proposed basis, they are looking at the
      effect of the change on a range of customer modelling points. One of these model
      points is for a new policy with an initial basic sum assured of £50,000 and a term of
      5 years.

      (ii)    Calculate, showing your workings, the regular bonuses attaching each year
              and the terminal bonus rate for this model point on both the current compound
              and proposed super-compound basis.                                         [7]

      (iii)   Comment on the results of the calculations carried out in part (ii) above.    [3]
                                                                                     [Total 13]
