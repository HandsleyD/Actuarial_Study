1    A life insurance company offers without-profits endowment assurances. Most
     business is sold in its home country but it also recently began selling lower volumes
     of similar products in two other countries.

     (i)      Explain how the company would determine best estimate mortality
              assumptions for its without-profits endowment assurance business.              [9]

     (ii)     Describe how the company’s mortality assumptions to determine supervisory
              reserves and capital requirements may differ from the best estimate
              assumptions.                                                               [4]
                                                                                  [Total 13]


2    A life insurance company launched a whole life assurance product 1 year ago and has
     just completed the first full experience review of the product.

     (i)      Explain the importance of experience monitoring in this scenario.              [6]

     (ii)     Identify the areas of experience that should be included in this review,
              indicating for each how the experience data may be subdivided.                 [7]

     (iii)    Explain why the company would carry out an analysis of surplus in this
              scenario.                                                                 [2]
                                                                                 [Total 15]


3    A life insurance company offers without-profits endowment assurance contracts
     where premiums and benefits are expressed as a fixed monetary amount.

     (i)      Describe the inflation risks to the life insurance company and to policyholders
              in respect of this policy.                                                   [5]

     (ii)     Describe how the inflation risks to the life insurance company and to
              policyholders differ if the benefits are instead linked to an inflation index. [2]
                                                                                        [Total 7]


4    A life insurance company offers both level and inflation-linked annuity products. In
     the country in which it operates, a market consistent valuation method is used.

     Suggest possible reasons why, when valuing its liabilities, the life insurance company
     does not take credit for an illiquidity premium on 100% of those liabilities.       [8]




SP2 A2025–2
5    A bank purchases the following term assurance products from a life insurance
     company:

           group term assurance (provided to all bank employees as part of their benefits
            package)
           individual term assurance (offered by the bank to its employees as an optional
            benefit)
           key person term assurance cover (to protect the bank in case of the death of one of
            the bank’s directors)
           cover for outstanding balances on unsecured loans issued to customers (to protect
            the bank in case a borrower dies before repaying a loan).

     For the following types of underwriting, compare the extent of underwriting that
     would be carried out by the life insurance company for each of the four products sold:

     (i)       medical underwriting                                                         [7]

     (ii)      financial underwriting                                                       [4]

     (iii)     claims underwriting.                                                         [4]
                                                                                     [Total 15]


6    A life insurance company has taken out original terms quota share reinsurance on its
     term assurance business.

     The reinsurer pays commission to the life insurance company on terms the life
     insurance company considers to be favourable.

     (i)       Explain how the reinsurer still expects to make a profit on this business.   [5]

     The life insurance company is considering increasing the quota share percentage
     ceded to the reinsurer given the favourable terms of its current reinsurance
     arrangement.

     (ii)      Suggest possible reasons why the life insurance company may decide not to
               increase the percentage ceded.                                            [4]
                                                                                  [Total 9]




SP2 A2025–3
7    A life insurance company has a portfolio of 500 identical policies that all commenced
     on 1 January a number of years ago, each with a guaranteed sum assured payable on
     death of £30,000.

     The per policy asset share at the start of the current year prior to premiums being paid
     was £3,476.20. Premiums are paid annually on 1 January, are £548 per policy, and per
     policy expenses are £55 p.a. The actual mortality rate in the year is 0.002357 and the
     investment return is 3.5% p.a. Surrenders and bonuses are excluded.

     (i)       Calculate, showing your workings, the per policy asset share for this portfolio
               at the end of the current year.                                              [5]

     (ii)      Comment on your answer to part (i).                                           [2]

     (iii)     Explain the effect on the asset share of a group of with profit policies each
               time a policy is surrendered.                                                  [4]
                                                                                       [Total 11]


8    (i)       Describe the features of convertible term assurance and renewable term
               assurance products.                                                           [3]

     A life insurance company is considering the launch of two new insurance products
     targeting young professionals in their late 20s to early 30s.

           Product A is a convertible term assurance product.
           Product B is a renewable term assurance product.

     (ii)      Explain the reasons why the target market may be attracted to these products.
                                                                                          [3]

     (iii)     Evaluate, for each product, A and B, how it addresses the reasons identified in
               part (ii).                                                                   [4]

     The company has chosen to launch Product A.

     (iv)      Discuss the considerations the company will take into account when designing
               this product.                                                            [12]
