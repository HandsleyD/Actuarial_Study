1     A life insurance company recently introduced a new unit-linked single premium bond
      product, allowing investment in a range of unit-linked funds.

      The unit-linked funds offered are sustainable investment options designed to comply
      with common Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) standards. The company
      manages the unit-linked funds internally.

      The launch of the product was very successful, with new premium income in the first
      quarter after launch being ten times that expected.

      (i)     Outline the risks to the company that may have arisen as a result of this
              product launch.                                                               [6]

      (ii)    Suggest possible ways in which the company could manage the risks that you
              have identified in part (i).                                             [6]
                                                                               [Total 12]


2     A life insurance company that entered the term assurance market 3 years ago has sold
      policies exclusively via the internet, using simplified underwriting.

      The company’s actuarial department has just completed an initial analysis of the
      mortality experience on its term assurance portfolio. The results of this analysis
      indicate that mortality was substantially higher than assumed in the pricing basis.

      (i)     Discuss the possible reasons for mortality experienced on the term assurance
              business being higher than allowed for in the pricing basis.               [5]

      (ii)    Suggest possible actions that the company may take after receiving the
              indications of higher mortality from the initial analysis.                 [6]
                                                                                  [Total 11]


3     A life insurance company offers a unit-linked endowment assurance product in which
      policyholders can choose to invest in a variety of different unit-linked funds.

      One of the funds offered is currently expanding. A new policyholder has just
      purchased one of these products and selected this fund.

      (i)     Set out how the price paid per unit will be calculated.                       [9]

      Over the following weeks, unfavourable market movements cause the value of the
      assets underlying the fund to drop. The company experiences an increase in
      encashments, to the point that the number of units cancelled exceeds the number
      created for several days in a row.

      (ii)    Discuss how the company may now calculate the price that new policyholders
              should pay when purchasing units.                                       [6]
                                                                               [Total 15]

4     A life insurance company allows policy alterations on its conventional whole life and
      endowment assurance business using the equating policy values method.

      (i)       Describe how the equating policy values method of alterations works.           [2]

      The company has just concluded a review of expenses and is concerned that the
      overall costs of alterations is too high. To discourage alterations, it is considering
      introducing new alteration terms.

      Under the new terms:

            the altered policy would be valued at 50% of its pre-alteration value.
            the company would retain the remaining 50% of the pre-alteration policy value.

      This contrasts with the company’s existing terms where the policy value is unchanged
      by the alteration.

      (ii)      Discuss the likely impact of these new terms on the company.                  [8]
                                                                                       [Total 10]

      (ii)      Discuss the assumptions that the life insurance company could sensitivity test

      (i)       Outline the actions that the policyholder may consider in relation to this

      portfolio of unit-linked endowment assurance products over a specific period. Data
      has been provided on both the policy movements and the movements on the

      (i)     Outline the checks that should be performed on the data that has been

      The analysis has revealed higher than expected lapses in this portfolio of business.

      (ii)    Outline the actions the life insurance company could take to reduce the level

8     A life insurance company sells whole life products through insurance intermediaries
      as well as its own direct sales force. The company is evaluating the efficiency of its
      sales methods with respect to converting prospective customers into a sale.

      (i)     Discuss how the likelihood of a consumer to purchase a life insurance product
              could differ depending on which of the two sales channels listed above is used.
                                                                                          [4]

      (ii)    For each of the following factors, outline the differences you would expect to
              see between business sold through insurance intermediaries compared to
              business sold via the direct sales force:

                 mortality rates
                 expenses and commission
                 persistency rates.
                                                                                               [9]

      A policyholder applied for whole of life cover via an insurance intermediary, then
      made a separate application for the same sum assured via the company’s direct sales
      force. The premiums quoted each time were different.

      (iii)   Outline the reasons, other than the factors identified in part (ii), for these
              premiums being different between the two sales channels for the same
              proposed sum assured.                                                             [5]
