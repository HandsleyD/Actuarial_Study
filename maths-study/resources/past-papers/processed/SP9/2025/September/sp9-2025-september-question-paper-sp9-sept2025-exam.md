1     Country Z is a large developed country. Company A is a well-established organisation
      in Country Z. Company A has a strong brand identity and a positive reputation for
      providing a range of products and services aimed at the senior population (defined as
      aged 50–75 years old) in Country Z. Company A is a publicly listed company, which
      wholly owns several subsidiaries. Company A generates revenue by receiving
      dividends from these subsidiaries and giving the subsidiaries in return the right to use
      Company A’s brand identity to facilitate businesses growth and to attract customers.
      The number of staff in Company A is relatively small compared to its subsidiaries.

      The wholly owned subsidiaries of Company A are as follows:

            Subsidiary B provides holiday packages in Country Z aimed at the senior
             population.
            Subsidiary C provides funeral plan insurance to the senior population, which pays
             a lump sum on death.
            Subsidiary D is a fashion brand providing clothes aimed at the senior population.
            Subsidiary E is a social network platform popular among the senior population.

      The risk management function of Company A is led by the Chief Risk Officer (CRO)
      who reports into Company A’s board. Each month the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
      of each subsidiary submits a report to Company A’s CRO, highlighting the top three
      risks of that subsidiary. Company A’s CRO uses these reports to provide regular
      updates to Company A’s board.

      (i)       Discuss the advantages and disadvantages to Company A of its corporate and
                risk management structure.                                               [6]

      (ii)      Describe some potential actions that can be taken to make improvements to
                the corporate structure.                                                  [6]

      (iii)     Explain how the opportunities and risks faced by subsidiaries B and E may
                interact.                                                                 [4]

      (iv)      Discuss how the diversification among Company A’s subsidiaries may affect
                its financial performance.                                              [4]

      A prominent financial commentator has recently published a magazine article on the
      performance of Company A. The article praised the success of Company A, in
      particular the value of its brand name and reputation.

      (v)       (a)     Set out the key risks that may impact the value of Company A’s brand
                        and reputation.                                                    [4]

                (b)     Suggest, for each risk in part (a), a mitigating action that Company A
                        could take.                                                           [2]

      The remuneration committee of Company A’s board is tasked with providing a
      proposal for the annual bonus to be awarded to each of the subsidiaries’ CEOs. One
      member of the committee has suggested that each CEO’s annual bonus should be
      linked directly to the level of dividend paid by that subsidiary to Company A during
      the previous year.

      (vi)      Comment on the appropriateness of the committee member’s suggestion,
                including consideration of alternative remuneration approaches.               [7]

      A popular television show in Country Z follows well-known celebrities as they
      research their family history. The show has created a growing interest among the
      senior population of Country Z in researching their own family history. Company A is
      considering setting up a new service that allows its customers to research their own
      family history by:

            providing an online platform, which contains digital records of Country Z’s
             historical census data, to allow customers to find details of their own family
             history.
            providing a DNA analysis based on DNA samples and family history submitted
             by the customer. The sample will be stored and used to match other customers’
             DNA to identify any genealogical relationships.

      (vii)     Suggest five processes that Company A could follow to identify the risks
                associated with the family history service, giving a brief description for each.
                                                                                               [5]

      (viii) Discuss three key stakeholders that may be impacted if Company A sets up the
             family history service, including a description of the impact.           [6]

      Company A is considering two options for launching the new family history service:

      1. Creating a new standalone subsidiary to offer the service.
      2. Offering the service as part of subsidiary E.

      (ix)      Discuss the pros and cons of each of these two approaches.                    [4]

2     Company P is a large supermarket chain. Company X is a subsidiary of Company P
      and produces a range of wine and other alcoholic beverages. Company X has the third
      largest share in the local market for wine and other alcoholic beverages.

      Company P is in the process of acquiring another subsidiary, Company Y, which is an
      established company that operates in the same market as Company X. Company Y
      has the second largest market share for wine and other alcoholic beverages in the local
      market.

      Company P plans to acquire Company Y, and then plans to merge Company X and
      Company Y.

      (i)     Discuss four risks that Company P should consider when evaluating
              Company Y as an acquisition target, including potential mitigation actions
              for each risk.                                                             [10]

      (ii)    Discuss six risks that may arise due to the integration of Company X and
              Company Y following a merger, including potential mitigation actions for
              each risk.                                                               [15]

      As part of the plan to merge Company X and Company Y, Company P is proposing to
      offer shares in Company P to the management of Company Y. These shares must be
      held for at least 3 years after the date of the merger.

      (iii)   Discuss the likely purpose of this proposal, including advantages and
              disadvantages to Company P.                                                 [5]

      Company X and Company Y are significantly smaller than Company P, each having a
      turnover of less than 1% of Company P’s turnover. Both Company X and Company Y
      currently supply wine and other alcoholic beverages to Company P and to other
      retailers in the local market.

      Company P has an established Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework,
      which includes procedures, guidelines and reporting requirements. To date, Company
      X has not adopted this ERM framework. However, in light of the potential acquisition
      of Company Y and the merger of Company X and Company Y, a member of
      Company P’s board has proposed that both Company X and Company Y adopt the
      ERM framework in its entirety.

      (iv)    Discuss the appropriateness of the board member’s proposal.                 [8]
