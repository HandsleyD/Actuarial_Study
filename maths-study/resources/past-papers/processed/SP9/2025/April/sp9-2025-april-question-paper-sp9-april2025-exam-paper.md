1    Company ABC (ABC) is an international corporation that owns a group of
     companies.

     (i)       Discuss the benefits to ABC of managing its risks in a holistic manner as a
               group of companies.                                                         [6]

     ABC owns a bank (‘the Bank’). The Bank owns an insurance subsidiary (‘the
     Subsidiary’). The Bank and the Subsidiary are related as follows:

     A The Bank fully owns the Subsidiary, as the sole shareholder.
     B The Bank provides banking services to the Subsidiary.
     C The Bank markets the Subsidiary’s products to its customers.
     D The Bank provides risk management and information technology services to the
       Subsidiary via a service level agreement.
     E The Bank purchases insurance cover from the Subsidiary as required to manage its
       risks.

     All transactions between the Bank and the Subsidiary are performed at arms’ length,
     so that each of the Bank and the Subsidiary act independently in their own best
     interest.

     The Subsidiary is concerned about the risks that may arise from the relationships that
     it holds with the Bank. The Subsidiary decides to appoint an actuary to produce a risk
     report. For each of the relationships A to E outlined above, the actuary’s report should
     identify:

           the risks to the Subsidiary arising from the relationship.
           the risk measurement tools used to assess each risk arising.
           proposed actions to mitigate each risk.

     (ii)      Set out the points that the actuary should include in their report.       [15]

     The Bank currently operates out of physical branches located in one country and does
     not offer internet banking.

     The Bank is proposing to expand into the electronic money (e-money) industry.
     E-money is a digital alternative to cash, which allows users to make cashless
     payments and undertake international transfers, with money stored on a card or
     mobile phone, or over the internet.

     (iii)     Discuss how ABC can apply the risk management control cycle to manage
               the risks associated with this proposal.                              [5]

     (iv)      Identify the risks arising to the Bank and ABC from expanding into the
               e-money industry.                                                           [6]

2    Sam and Alex are qualified actuaries who live together and work at different
     companies. Together, Sam and Alex own a house in their home country plus an
     additional overseas property. Sam and Alex’s house has an outstanding mortgage for

     its value. The overseas property is let to a long-term tenant, in a country that uses a
     different currency to Sam and Alex’s home country.

     Sam and Alex wish to purchase a third property in their home country, which will
     serve as a holiday home. When Sam and Alex are not using the holiday home, they
