1     For a particular class of business, the full credibility standard is achieved when there
      are 100 policies and the square root rule is used for partial credibility.

      The partial credibility calculated using the Bühlmann–Straub method is equal to the
      square root rule when there are 25 policies.

      Determine the Bühlmann–Straub credibility factor under the full credibility standard.

2     An insurance company selling homeowners’ insurance uses Artificial Intelligence
      (AI) in its policy application process as follows:

      The homeowner takes a specified number of photographs of the outside and inside of
      their home and uploads them to the insurance company’s system. This system uses AI
      to automatically collect various data points from the uploaded photographs and
      provides a quote.

      (i)     List the data that the AI system may collect from the photographs to provide
              quotations.                                                                 [4]

      The insurer wants to roll out a similar AI system to provide quotes for commercial
      properties.

      (ii)    Suggest challenges that the insurer may face while using this AI system to
              quote for commercial policies.                                              [6]

      (ii)    Outline how the insurance company may address the issues in part (i).         [4]

4     A general insurance company, Company U, wants to launch a new product –
      emissions insurance. This product supports marine clients in addressing unforeseen
      additional carbon emissions on a journey.

      Emissions insurance will be an extension to an existing marine hull policy. In the
      event of an unforeseen deviation on a journey due to repairs, the insurance policy will
      pay out compensation in the form of the value of credits equivalent to the excess
      emissions arising.

      (i)     Discuss the reasons for Company U wishing to launch this emissions
              insurance product.                                                            [4]

      To calculate the risk premium on each policy, Company U will collect data on the
      total excess emissions and the annual revenue for the marine clients over the past
      2 years.

      (ii)    Comment on the limitations in using this data.                                [4]

      machine learning techniques in the pricing model instead of a traditional GLM

      (ii)    Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of this proposal, from the viewpoint
