2     A general insurance company provides a rainfall index insurance product that is
      aimed at wheat farmers with low income in a particular region. If rainfall for the rainy
      season falls below a 40 mm trigger, then farmers would be paid €20 for every 5 mm
      below the trigger for every acre of land. In exchange, the farmers would pay a regular
      affordable premium for the duration of the policy.

      (i)       Describe how pricing for this rainfall index insurance product may differ to
                a traditional general insurance product.                                     [8]

      (ii)      Outline the advantages and disadvantages of this product from the perspective
                of the general insurance company.                                          [8]
