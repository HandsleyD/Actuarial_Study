2     A large health and care insurance company has been writing an individual pre-funded
      Long-Term Care Insurance (LTCI) product for many years. Policyholders pay a
      regular premium that is reviewable every 5 years. In return, policyholders receive a
      fixed regular income in the event of sickness or disability, payable for life or until
      earlier recovery. The policy includes a waiver of the premium while the benefit is
      being paid.

      The next 5-year premium review is now due.

      (i)     Describe how the insurer would determine the following assumptions for the
              premium review exercise:

              (a)    Morbidity

              (b)    Mortality

              (c)    Lapses.
                                                                                           [12]

      (ii)    Outline how random fluctuations could impact the credibility of the insurer’s
              morbidity, mortality and lapse experience.                                  [3]
                                                                                   [Total 15]

4     In a particular country the only benefits provided by the government to citizens are
      free accident and emergency medical cover. Retirement benefits are provided to
      employed citizens through occupational pension schemes, which typically also
      provide lump sum benefits payable on death before retirement. There is a very active
      private health insurance sector with health insurers providing a full range of health
      insurance policies.

      Consider each of the following examples:

           A low-income family where both husband and wife are aged 25, are both
            employed and have no dependents
           A wealthy single woman aged 53 with no children
           A medium to high income family with two children, where both adults are aged 42
            with one in full-time employment and the other a full-time parent.

      (i)       Identify the likely health care needs for each example.                     [3]

      (ii)      Recommend suitable healthcare products to meet the needs identified in
                part (i) for each example.                                                [9]
                                                                                   [Total 12]


5     Company B is a health and care insurance company specialising in long-term critical
      illness insurance business.

      Company B currently reinsures a significant proportion of its critical illness business
      to external reinsurers. For a number of years the total premiums paid to the reinsurers
      by Company B has been higher than the corresponding reinsurance recoveries. The
      Finance Director of Company B has expressed concerns that reinsurance does not
      represent good value for money for Company B.

      The Finance Director has asked an actuary to carry out an investigation into how
      reinsurance impacts the profitability of Company B’s critical illness business.

      (i)       Discuss how the actuary could carry out this investigation.                 [7]

      (ii)      Outline factors other than the impact on profitability that Company B should
                consider when deciding on whether to continue reinsuring a significant
                proportion of its critical illness business externally.                    [7]

      Having reviewed the actuary’s investigation, the Finance Director has agreed that it is
      appropriate to continue reinsuring a significant proportion of Company B’s critical
      illness business to external reinsurers. The Finance Director has asked if there are any
      actions which could be taken to improve the cost effectiveness of Company B’s
      reinsurance arrangements.

      (iii)     Suggest possible actions Company B could take to make its existing
                reinsurance arrangements more cost effective.                               [6]
                                                                                     [Total 20]
