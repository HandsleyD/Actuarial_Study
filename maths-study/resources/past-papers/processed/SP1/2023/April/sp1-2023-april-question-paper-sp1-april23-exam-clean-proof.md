2    Company A is a health and care insurance company that specialises in individual and
     group Income Protection (IP) business in Country X. Company A’s product
     development team has proposed launching a new ‘accident only’ IP product that only
     provides regular benefit payments for a loss of earnings as a result of an accident.
     This type of ‘accident only’ IP product is not currently being offered by any of the
     health and care insurers in Country X, and market research conducted by the product
     development team indicated that an ‘accident only’ IP product has been very
     successful in a neighbouring country.

     (i)      Suggest possible reasons why an ‘accident only’ IP product was successful in
              the neighbouring country.                                                  [3]

     (ii)     Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of an ‘accident only’ IP product
              from the perspective of the customers.                                    [3]

     (iii)    Discuss the factors that Company A may consider in designing the product
              features for the proposed ‘accident only’ IP product.                   [10]

     (iv)     Discuss the factors that Company A may consider in setting the premium rates
              for the proposed ‘accident only’ IP product.                             [12]
                                                                                 [Total 28]

4    Company A is a large health and care insurance company that sells a full range of
     health and care products including:

     (a) individual standalone Critical Illness (CI) with tiered benefits.
     (b) Group Income Protection (GIP).
     (c) Group Private Medical Insurance and Individual Private Medical Insurance
         (GPMI and IPMI).

     (i)      Discuss the suitability of the various distribution channels for each of the
              health and care products above.

                 insurance intermediaries (brokers)
                 tied agents
                 own salesforce
                 direct marketing
                 worksite marketing.                                                        [11]

     The persistency experience of Company A’s individual Critical Illness (CI) business
     has been deteriorating in recent years. In response to this, it has been proposed that
     Company A will introduce a level commission system where regular level payments
     will be paid to the distributor over the policy’s premium paying term. This is to
     replace Company A’s existing indemnity commission system where a lump sum
     payment is paid to the distributor at policy commencement.

     (ii)     Discuss the factors that Company A may need to consider before adopting the
              proposed level commission system.                                        [5]

5    A health and care insurance company that specialises in individual and group Income
     Protection (IP) business has seen its new business volumes for individual IP business
     gradually declining over recent years. The Marketing Director has suggested that the
     concept of ‘free cover limits’ used for its group IP business should also be applied to
     its individual IP business, where full underwriting will only be conducted on new
     individual IP policies where the benefit amount chosen by the policyholders exceeds
     the ‘free cover limit’ for individual IP business.

     (i)      Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of the Marketing Director’s
              proposal from the perspective of the health and care insurance company.         [5]

     The company has decided to adopt a ‘free cover limit’ system for all its future new
     individual IP policies.

     (ii)     Suggest possible actions that the company may consider in order to address
              the disadvantages identified in part (i) above.                            [4]
