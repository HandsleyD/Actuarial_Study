CP3 S2024–2
Proposed changes to pricing approach
Following the request from the Insurance Oversight Board (note N38) the pricing team have
spent some time reviewing the pricing approach. They have paid particular attention to the
business interruption benefit of our policy, which is likely to see more claims from extreme
weather events. Below are the recommendations for a revised approach to better capture
emerging climate trends.

Recommendation 1

Prices will continue to be reviewed annually using generalised linear models. At the time of
each annual pricing review, the pricing committee will consider the risks from climate change
and ensure that these are reflected in the pricing approach. To better facilitate discussion on
these risks, the Climate Change Risk Manager will be appointed to the pricing committee.

Recommendation 2

An additional variable should be used when calculating premiums calculations. The new
variable is: X7 Flood zone.

Three flood zones will be used:

   Zone 1 – Low probability of flooding (land with less than 0.1% annual probability of
    river or coastal flooding).
   Zone 2 – Medium probability of flooding (land with between 0.1% and 1% annual
    probability of river flooding, or between 0.1% and 0.5% annual probability of coastal
    flooding).
   Zone 3 – High probability of flooding (land with a greater than 1% annual probability of
    river flooding, or a greater than 0.5% annual probability of coastal flooding).

The flood zone for each policy will be determined from location data, for example postcode,
already supplied by the intermediary. The pricing algorithm will look up the applicable flood
zone from the Local Flood Authority (LFA) database. The LFA regularly reviews the flood
zone data to reflect emerging flood risks.
