1    A company that sponsors a Defined Benefit (DB) pension scheme for its employees is
     concerned about the rising cost of financing the scheme. The company wishes to close
     the DB scheme to future accrual and introduce a new scheme that has a lower overall
     cost.

     (i)       Outline the advantages and disadvantages of the following types of pension
               scheme from the perspective of:

               (a)      the sponsoring employer.

               (b)      the employees.

                    DB
                    Defined Contribution (DC)
                    cash balance
                    collective defined contribution
                    defined contribution with DB underpin.
                                                                                           [14]

     The company proposes to close the DB scheme to future accrual and introduce a new
     DC scheme. This new DC scheme will provide future accrual for active DB scheme
     members and for new employees.

     (ii)      Discuss how this proposal would impact the total retirement benefits from
               both schemes for:

                    existing members of the DB scheme.
                    future new employees.
                                                                                             [8]

     The company has notified the DB scheme’s trustees of their proposal. Before they
     provide a response, the trustees have asked their actuary to provide advice that:

           identifies any concerns and issues in relation to the closure.
           discusses possible actions (both immediate and longer term) which the trustees
            may take following closure.

     (iii)     Discuss the factors that the trustees should consider in relation to the scheme
               closure.                                                                      [3]

     (iv)      Suggest possible actions (both immediate and longer term) which the trustees
               may take following the closure.                                            [7]

     The DB scheme is closed and all active members have become deferred pensioners.
     The company is now looking at ways to reduce the size of the DB scheme and to
     increase the assets invested in the DC scheme.

     The following member offers have been proposed:

           Offer deferred members of the DB scheme the option to transfer their accrued
            benefits into the DC scheme. Members taking up this option would be given an
            enhancement to the amount transferred.
           Offer pensioner members of the DB scheme the option to transfer the present
            value of their future pension increases into the DC scheme. The pension in the DB
            scheme would no longer be subject to increases and the fund transferred into the
            DC scheme would be accessed via drawdown as and when the member chooses.

     (v)       Describe the considerations that the company will need to take into account in
               making these offers to members and the practicalities of implementing them.
                                                                                         [12]

     The company decides to proceed with both member offers.

     (vi)      Discuss the factors that the deferred and pensioner members should take into
               account when deciding whether to take up the offers made to them.          [8]

2    The funding valuation for a mature final salary scheme, Scheme A, which is closed to
     accrual, has just started. Scheme A’s trustees have requested some training on
     valuation assumptions and have asked you to produce a paper setting out the main
     assumptions that will be required for the valuation and the relative importance of each
     assumption.

     (i)      Outline the main points that you would include in your paper. You do not need
              to include details of how the assumptions would be derived.                [8]

     Your paper is discussed at a trustee meeting. One of the trustees responds by saying:

     ‘An actuary I know told me that the only thing you can guarantee about assumptions
     is that they will be wrong, which means we will be funding for the wrong level of
     benefits.’

     (ii)     Set out the points you would make in your reply to the trustee.               [6]

     Scheme A currently uses a separate discount rate pre- and post-retirement. One of the
     trustees has asked why the rates are different. They have also suggested that it may be
     more appropriate to use yield curves to set the discount rate.

     (iii)    (a)    Explain why different discount rates are used pre- and post-retirement.
                                                                                         [3]

              (b)    Set out how a yield curve approach for setting the discount rate would
                     work.                                                                [3]

              (c)    Outline the advantages and disadvantages of the yield curve approach
                     in part (iii)(b) compared to the current method.                   [4]

     Scheme A’s sponsor is a manufacturing company, Company A, which makes
     components for the electronics industry. A recent scientific discovery has provided
     evidence to show that one of the raw materials that has been used by the company for
     decades causes cancer in people who have had long-term exposure to it.

     (iv)     Discuss how this discovery may affect the scheme’s funding valuation
              assumptions.                                                                  [7]

     Company A operates two factories in different locations, producing different items
     from its product lines. Company A has had an offer from a competitor, Company B,
     to purchase the smaller of the two factories and take over the staff located at that
     factory who make up 15% of the sponsor’s total workforce.

     Company B sponsors a final salary scheme, Scheme B. Scheme A’s trustees have
     asked you to advise them on the options for calculating an amount to be paid into
     Scheme B to transfer the relevant staff members’ benefits.

     (v)      Identify two possible approaches to calculating a suitable value to transfer into
              Scheme B.                                                                      [1]
