     A life insurance company in Territory X offers a unit-linked individual personal

3    A small life insurance company runs a with-profits fund that consists of the following:

          A mature block of conventional with-profits endowment policies, which provide a
           cash fund at retirement, with the option to purchase an annuity with the company at
           a guaranteed minimum annuity rate. Annuities purchased are written outside the
           with-profits fund. This contract is no longer sold.
          A more recent block of unitised accumulating with-profits contracts, which are still
           sold. There is no guaranteed annuity rate under this contract.

     Both the method of distributing surplus under with-profits contracts and the insurance
     regulations governing solvency valuations are similar to those in the UK.

     The company is anticipating a period of adverse economic conditions. The company
     has carried out a stress testing exercise to understand the potential financial impact on
     the company’s with-profits fund of a sudden economic shock. The stress being
     considered is made up of a combination of the following elements:

            Interest rates           Reduction of 100 bps (1%) at all durations
            Corporate bonds      Increase in spread of 200 bps (2%) at all durations
            Equity/property                25% reduction in market value
            Currency                   10% fall in value of domestic currency

     The company defines the estate of the with-profits fund as the difference between the
     assets in the fund less the technical provisions. It also defines the required capital for
     the with-profits fund to be equivalent to the SCR that the with-profits fund would be
     required to hold if it were a stand-alone company.

     (i)        Describe the impacts of the combined stress outlined above on the estate and
                required capital of the company’s with-profits fund, prior to allowing for any
                management actions.                                                         [13]

     (ii)       Discuss any management actions relating to the with-profits fund the company
                may want to incorporate into the scenario.                               [17]

     Two years later, a shock has taken place, but since then market conditions have
     improved. This recovery combined with the company’s management actions have
     taken the with-profits fund’s estate into a position where it exceeds the upper limit of
     the company’s preferred range for the estate.

     The company has therefore decided to distribute some of the surplus to with-profits
     policyholders to restore it to a level within the preferred range.

     (iii)      Discuss the factors the company should consider when deciding how to make
                an appropriate distribution of surplus.                                [13]
