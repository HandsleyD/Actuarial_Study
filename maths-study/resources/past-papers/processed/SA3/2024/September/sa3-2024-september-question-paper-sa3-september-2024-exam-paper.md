1    Company A is an established commercial lines insurance company operating in a
     developed market.

     Recently, Company A introduced a new product that provides insurance cover for
     wind turbines. The product insures both onshore and offshore wind turbine
     installations.

     (i)      List examples of the insured perils for this product.                         [3]

     (ii)     Outline rating factors that can be used in the pricing of this new product.   [3]

     (iii)    Describe how an actuary can be involved in the pricing of this product.       [3]

     The book has grown significantly over the past few years. However, it has not been
     profitable and claims experience has been volatile.

     (iv)     Suggest possible actions Company A could take to improve the profitability
              of this product.                                                           [4]

     (v)      Explain the types of reinsurance arrangements that may be suitable for this
              product.                                                                    [3]

     The reserving actuary from Company A provides a range of possible reserve results
     for the insurance product for wind turbines. However, the Chief Financial Officer
     (CFO) for Company A has asked for a point estimate instead.

     (vi)     Discuss the CFO’s request.                                                    [6]

     One of the directors from Company A has said that they are concerned about the
     impact of extreme weather events on the profitability of the book. Therefore, the
     director has suggested that the company stops writing the business until the risks are
     better understood.

     (vii)    Discuss the director’s suggestion.                                            [8]

2    Company Z is a large personal lines insurer that has been operating for many years in
     a mature general insurance market. The company is looking to upgrade its current
     claims handling process to improve its customer experience.

     (i)      Give examples of features that are desirable in a claims handling process.   [3]

     Company Z is considering implementing a new claims handling process based on
     machine learning and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

     (ii)     Describe how the use of machine learning and AI may improve Company Z’s
              claims handling process.                                             [6]

     (iii)    Discuss how this new claims handling process may impact:

              (a)    reserving.                                                            [5]

              (b)    pricing and capital.                                                  [4]

     (iv)     Describe how other areas of the business may be impacted by this new claims
              handling process.                                                         [6]

     The insurance regulator is concerned that the new system may be detrimental for the
     customers as it may result in some customers being unfairly discriminated against.

     (v)      Discuss the regulator’s concern.                                            [4]

3    Company K is a large established multinational general insurance company that
     writes a range of general insurance products. The company has a financial model and
     has recently obtained approval from the insurance regulator to use a full internal
     model for its calculations of regulatory capital.

     (i)      List the uses of a financial model in an insurance company.                  [3]

     The catastrophe model component of the internal model is provided by an external
     vendor.

     The company recently suffered significant losses from a catastrophe event. The losses
     primarily came from a region-peril that was not captured in the catastrophe model
     component of the internal model.

     (ii)     List examples of the type of metrics that can be obtained from the capital
              model.                                                                       [3]

     (iii)    Give possible reasons why the specific region-peril was not modelled in the
              vendor’s model.                                                             [4]

     The insurance regulator has told Company K that its model needs to be updated to
     better reflect its risks. For this, the company is planning on using an updated
     catastrophe model from its external vendor. However, it will not be released in time
     for Company K’s next regulatory capital submission.

     (iv)     Suggest what Company K can do for this next regulatory submission, given
              the updated model will not be ready in time.                             [4]

     (v)      Suggest ways that Company K can validate the updated catastrophe model.
                                                                                           [5]

     The chief actuary of Company K also attributes the unexpected losses to climate
     change. They said that more work needs to be done so that the model better captures
     climate change risks across the business.

     (vi)     Discuss the challenges with reflecting the impact of climate change in the
              capital model.                                                             [6]

     The CFO of Company K said that making changes to the model is unnecessary as it
     will lead to higher regulatory capital requirements.

     (vii)    Comment on the statement made by the CFO.                                    [6]
