3    (i)      List the benefits to a general insurance company of expanding the sale of
              insurance policies to another country.                                       [2]

     A general insurance company, Company Z, which has operated for a number of years
     in Country A has decided to expand its insurance business into Country B and is
     considering various options for this expansion.

     (ii)     Describe different strategies that Company Z can take to start selling insurance
              policies in a foreign country.                                                [6]

     Country A has a highly developed insurance market. The majority of insurance
     policies sold in Country A are sold online either directly from insurers’ own websites
     or via aggregator websites. Country B has a less developed insurance market, with all
     insurance policies sold in person via insurance agents.

     (iii)    Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of aggregator sites from the
              perspective of an insurance company.                                          [3]

     (iv)     Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of an insurance company selling
              its products through insurance agents from the perspective of a customer. [3]

     (v)      Discuss how different distribution channels will impact the pricing of policies
              that Company Z can sell in Country A and Country B.                          [5]

     Country A’s currency is different to Country B’s currency. Company Z’s business
     is denominated in the currency of the country that the business was written in.
     Company Z uses central actuarial and finance teams based in Country A that
     cover operations in both countries.

     (vi)     Discuss the impact of working with different currencies on:

              (a)    reserving.                                                            [2]

              (b)    pricing.                                                               [2]

              (c)    capital modelling.                                                     [2]

     (vii)    Outline other areas of Company Z that are impacted by business being written
              in different currencies.                                                  [3]
