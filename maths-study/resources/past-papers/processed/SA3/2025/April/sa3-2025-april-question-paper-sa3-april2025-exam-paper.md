2    (i)      Outline the three main types of risks associated with climate change for
              insurance companies.                                                             [3]

     Country A is a large, geographically diverse country. A significant natural catastrophe
     event has hit Country A in recent months. This has caused a significant impact to the
     country’s economy. These events have become more frequent in recent years. As a
     result, the financial regulator in Country A became concerned about the impact of
     climate change on the country’s economy and on the insurance industry.

     Company Y is a large insurance and reinsurance company writing a variety of
     products and operating primarily in Country A. The chief risk officer of Company Y
     has requested a report on the impact of climate change on Company Y.

     (ii)     Discuss the impact of climate change on Company Y’s:

              (a)    pricing.

              (b)    reserving.

              (c)    capital modelling.
                                                                                               [9]

     The financial regulator of Country A has instructed all insurance companies in
     Country A to prepare a report detailing the possible impacts of climate change on
     their short-term and long-term financial stability under a number of potential
     scenarios.

     (iii)    Discuss the possible climate-related scenarios and their impact that
              Company Y should consider and include in its report.                             [8]

     As a result of extensive investigations undertaken by the insurance industry to assess
     the impact of climate change, it was projected that the future claims will increase
     significantly. As a consequence, the cost of both personal and commercial insurance
     has increased significantly and, in some cases, the insurance became unavailable.

     (iv)     Suggest possible ways in which the government of Country A can improve the

4    Company ABC sells the following types of insurance products to consumers through
     affinity relationships with car dealers:

     •      extended warranty insurance (EW)
     •      gap (residual value) insurance (GAP)
     •      payment protection insurance (PPI).

     (i)       Describe the key cover provided under each of these products.               [6]

     The regulator has expressed concern that products of this nature may provide poor
     value for consumers, particularly when sold through affinity sales channels alongside
     major purchases such as motor vehicles.

     (ii)      Suggest possible reasons why the regulator may be concerned that these
               products sold through this sales channel may provide poor value for
               consumers.                                                                  [8]

     Following a thematic review into these concerns, the regulator has identified a
     number of areas where consumers were treated unfairly and imposed new
     requirements for any insurers operating in these markets to demonstrate that they are
     providing sufficient value to consumers.

     The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Company ABC has requested a new
     monitoring process is introduced to ensure that these products deliver value for
     consumers.

     (iii)     Suggest six possible metrics that could be used in the requested monitoring
               process, with reasons why they may indicate if consumers are getting
               sufficient value from an insurance product.                                 [6]

     Following the regulator’s thematic review, some insurers involved in this market are
     expected to need to pay substantial compensation claims to policyholders.

     The CEO has asked the actuarial team to build a model to explore the potential range
     of compensation payments that Company ABC may need to make.

     (iv)      Outline the factors that may be considered in this model for compensation
