1    An insurance company has identified a significant valuation surplus on one of its
     product lines. It is considering investing a proportion of the surplus in high-yield
     bonds to increase returns.

     (i)    Set out how the insurance company could determine the proportion of the
            surplus that could be invested in high yield bonds.                     [3]

     (ii)   Suggest other possible uses for the surplus.                                    [3]
                                                                                      [Total 6]


2    A consultancy has decided to replace the model it uses to value its clients’ liabilities.

     (i)    Suggest possible reasons why the consultancy may want to replace its current
            valuation model.                                                          [3]

     The consultancy has decided to purchase a model from an external provider. It is
     aiming to move all its clients onto the new model within 3 months.

     (ii)   Describe:

            (a)     the possible challenges that the company may encounter in
                    implementing the new model.

            (b)     how the challenges may be overcome.
                                                                                            [4]
                                                                                      [Total 7]


3    A mutual insurance company needs to improve its free capital position as shown on
     its regulatory balance sheet.

     (i)    Outline how debt could be used to improve the free capital position.            [4]

     (ii)   Outline possible management actions the company could take to improve its
            free capital position.                                                   [3]
                                                                              [Total 7]


4    A financial adviser has been asked to provide a 1-hour presentation on personal
     finance aimed at 15-year-olds.

     (i)    Outline the important topics that the financial adviser could include in the
            presentation.                                                                [4]

     (ii)   Discuss how the presentation would differ if it was a 3-hour presentation
            aimed at 60-year-olds.                                                       [4]
                                                                                   [Total 8]

5    A bank only sells mortgages to individuals with lower-than-average income. It has
     historically been a successful business, but last year it saw its profits fall.

     (i)     Suggest possible reasons why the bank’s profits may have fallen last year. [4]

     (ii)    Identify ratios that the bank could use to explain the reasons for the fall in
             profits.                                                                       [2]

     Due to the fall in its profits last year, the bank, as part of its annual report, has added
     commentary on the main reasons that its profitability fell over the past year.

     (iii)   Outline the types of information that would be included in this commentary.
                                                                                        [3]
                                                                                  [Total 9]


6    (i)     List the demographic assumptions that a defined benefit pension scheme could
             make in its funding valuation basis.                                     [3]

     (ii)    Explain the reasons a benefit scheme may use in each of the assumptions in
             part (i).                                                                  [4]

     (iii)   Compare the level of detail that would be used in setting the demographic
             assumptions for valuing a small scheme as opposed to a very large scheme.
                                                                                              [3]
                                                                                       [Total 10]


7    (i)     Explain why a financial institution may want to securitise assets.                [3]

     (ii)    List the stakeholders for this securitisation.                                    [3]

     (iii)   Discuss the issues that a financial organisation may face when marketing a
             securitisation of a portfolio.                                              [3]
                                                                                   [Total 9]

8    The banking regulator in a particular country has recently reviewed a bank and has
     requested that the bank reviews its existing risk management processes.

     (i)     Explain the benefits to the bank of having effective risk management
             processes.                                                                      [3]

     (ii)    Suggest possible reasons for the regulator’s request.                           [3]

     (iii)   Outline how each of the following individuals can be part of the bank’s risk
             management process:

             (a)    a junior analyst in the bank’s trading department

             (b)    the bank’s chief risk officer

             (c)    an institutional shareholder that has a large holding of shares in the
                    bank.
                                                                                             [3]

     (iv)    Outline the information that may be included in the risk reporting section of a
             bank’s published annual report.                                              [2]
                                                                                   [Total 11]


9    A defined benefit pension scheme includes a lump sum death benefit for members.
     This benefit, equal to four times salary, is paid if the member dies before retirement.
     The scheme has not insured this benefit, therefore the lump sum is paid directly from
     the scheme’s funds.

     Over the last year, the mortality rate of members before retirement was approximately
     twice the mortality rate assumed in the scheme’s most recent valuation.

     (i)     Describe how the scheme managers could use the results of their actuary’s
             analysis to understand the implications of mortality experience of
             pre-retirement members.                                                   [6]

     The scheme’s managers are deciding whether to take out insurance for the cost of the
     lump sum death benefits.

     (ii)    Outline the main factors that the managers should take into account in making
             their decision.                                                             [5]
                                                                                  [Total 11]

     that start before state pension age. Instead, it will require citizens to purchase
     insurance products that will provide similar benefits. It is expected that a number of
     insurers will offer these insurance products.

     (i)    Outline four insurance products that could provide similar benefits to a
            government’s social security system for individuals below state pension age.
                                                                                       [6]

     (ii)   Discuss the advantages to a government of introducing a compulsory
            requirement for individuals to purchase long-term disability insurance.     [4]
                                                                                 [Total 10]


11   An insurance company offers a range of products including income protection
     insurance, term assurance and home contents insurance (which includes lost or
     stolen items from the insured’s person as well as from their home).

     (i)    Describe, for each of these products, sources of fraud risk to the insurer.   [6]

     (ii)   Suggest, for each of the products, how the insurance company can protect
            itself against each fraud risk identified in part (i).                    [6]
