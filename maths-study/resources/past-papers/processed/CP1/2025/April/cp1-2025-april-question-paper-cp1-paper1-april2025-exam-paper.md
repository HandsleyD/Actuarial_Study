1    A developed country has a large liquid government bond market and has issued both
     conventional and index-linked bonds. The difference between the nominal yield curve
     for conventional government bonds and the real yield curve for index-linked
     government bonds is an implied measure of future inflation.

     Discuss the appropriateness of an insurance company using this implied measure of
     future inflation when valuing different types of inflation risk exposure in its assets
     and liabilities.                                                                       [6]


2    A government in a developing country is considering introducing new disclosure
     requirements for companies to start showing the value of any benefit schemes they
     operate for their employees in their accounts.

     Discuss the advantages of the new disclosure requirements for:

           potential buyers of a company.
           members of the benefit scheme.
           the government.
                                                                                           [6]


3    A life insurance company requires potential policyholders of its long-term sickness
     policies to undergo medical underwriting on application.

     (i)       Describe the advantages to the company of medically underwriting potential
               policyholders.                                                            [3]

     (ii)      Outline possible ways that the insurance company can manage morbidity risk
               after policy inception.                                                  [3]
                                                                                 [Total 6]


4    A company selling motor insurance has recently moved to algorithmic decision
     making (i.e. automated decisions made without human intervention using a computer
     algorithm) for underwriting and pricing their policies. The company has seen a
     significant increase in the number of customer complaints since making this change.

     (i)       Suggest reasons why moving to algorithmic decision making for underwriting
               and pricing may have increased the number of customer complaints.       [5]

     (ii)      Suggest two other ways the company could use algorithmic decision making.
                                                                                        [2]
                                                                                  [Total 7]

5    A well-established general insurance company is considering launching a new pet
     insurance product. The product will cover veterinary bills on illness or injury of the
     insured pet.

     (i)     Set out how the insurance company may determine the theoretical cost of
             this product.                                                                    [3]

     (ii)    Suggest reasons why individuals may purchase this product.                    [6]
                                                                                     [Total 9]


6    The regulator of defined benefit pension funds in a developed country has decided
     that pension funds are not taking risk management sufficiently seriously. The
     regulator has requested that all pension funds carry out a risk assessment of their
     funds and any related documentation produced for the fund.

     (i)     Outline how pension funds may quantify the risks they are exposed to.            [3]

     (ii)    List four approaches that could be used to model risks of pension funds.         [2]

     (iii)   Discuss how the correlations between the risks of a pension fund can be
             monitored.                                                                       [2]

     (iv)    Outline how key risks of a pension fund can be mitigated.                    [3]
                                                                                   [Total 10]


7    A life insurance company sells term assurances.

     The company is considering offering tailored term assurance premium rates to the
     customers of a bank that serves only high-wealth individuals.

     (i)     Explain why the insurer’s own data may not be appropriate for setting the
             mortality assumptions for the tailored premium rates.                     [6]

     (ii)    State, with reasons, external data sources that the insurer could use when
             setting the mortality assumptions for the tailored premium rates.             [4]
                                                                                    [Total 10]


8    A large defined benefit pension scheme has its actuarial, investment and
     administration services provided by an in-house department.

     The sponsoring employer has suggested that it may be more cost effective to
     outsource these services to external parties. The employer has asked for an analysis
     of the expenses incurred by the in-house department.

     (i)     Set out how the expenses of the in-house department could be analysed.           [5]

     (ii)    Outline the possible advantages to the scheme and sponsoring employer of
             outsourcing these services to third parties.                             [5]
                                                                               [Total 10]

9    An organisation owns and manages six care homes that provide nursing care for
     around 50 elderly people in each home.

     The care homes charge the residents monthly fees. The fees cover the costs of
     accommodation, meals, laundry and heating, together with nursing and personal care.
     The fees are reviewed annually to take into account any increased costs. The
     organisation is proposing to introduce a guarantee to residents such that they can
     continue to stay in the care home even if they run out of funds and can no longer
     afford the fees.

     (i)    Discuss the risks involved with this proposal for:

            (a)     the organisation.

            (b)     the residents of the care homes.
                                                                                            [6]

     (ii)   Suggest how the organisation could manage the risks identified in part (i). [4]
                                                                                [Total 10]


10   An insurance company sells both life and general insurance products. In order to
     diversify its business further, it has just started selling a new insurance product.

     Having analysed the risks in the new product, the company decided to retain all of the
     insurance risks of the new product.

     (i)    Describe possible reasons for the company’s decision.                           [6]

     (ii)   Outline sources of operational risks to the company posed by the new product.
                                                                                       [5]
                                                                                [Total 11]




CP11 A2025–4
11   An established life insurance company with low free capital only sells immediate
     annuities.

     (i)     List the main factors influencing investment strategy for this company.        [4]

     (ii)    State the two main types of assets the company is likely to hold.              [1]

     To date, the regulations have required that the insurance company’s assets and
     liabilities are valued using a discount rate based on yields when the assets held to
     back the liabilities were purchased. This rate is not subsequently updated.

     From the next financial year end, all insurance companies will instead be required to
     value both assets and liabilities at market-based discount rates appropriate at the
     valuation date.

     (iii)   Describe the impacts this change in regulations is likely to have on the
             company.                                                                       [5]

     (iv)    Discuss possible reasons why the regulations have been changed.               [5]
                                                                                    [Total 15]
