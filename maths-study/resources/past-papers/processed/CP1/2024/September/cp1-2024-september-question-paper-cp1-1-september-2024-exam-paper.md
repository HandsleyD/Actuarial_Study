1    The insurance industry in a developed country has, for many years, been subject to
     regulations that require insurance companies to treat their customers fairly. The
     regulator has proposed a change to the treating customers fairly regulations that will
     impose a higher standard of customer care on insurance companies.

     Discuss why the regulator may have made this proposal.                                 [5]


2    A financial provider is developing a new pension product that will be targeted at
     individuals who have recently graduated from university.

     Describe the main factors the provider should consider when developing this new
     product.                                                                        [5]


3    A general insurance company selling only motor and home insurance business is
     concerned about its low solvency capital coverage.

     Suggest possible actions the general insurance company could take to improve its
     solvency position.                                                               [6]


4    Assess the effectiveness of using stochastic modelling for risk evaluation in the
     following products:

     (i)    A term assurance policy.                                                        [3]

     (ii)   A lump sum that is to be held by a company in a cash account for 10 years.
            After 10 years, the sum is paid into the company’s defined benefit scheme
            if that scheme is in deficit. If the scheme is not in deficit, then the lump sum
            will be refunded to the company.                                                 [3]
                                                                                       [Total 6]


5    A general insurance company is reviewing its model that estimates the costs of
     Incurred But Not Reported (IBNR) claims and has commissioned a consultancy to
     undertake a review of the model and, if required, recommend changes to the model.
     The consultancy has carried out its review and has recommended that the insurance
     company replace its existing IBNR model, with an IBNR model that the consultancy
     had previously built for one of the insurer’s competitors.

     Discuss the issues the insurance company will need to consider before it decides to
     use the IBNR model that the consultancy had developed for another client.           [7]




CP11 S2024–2
6    Five dentists are setting up a dental practice and have agreed on the fees to charge for
     each type of dental treatment. Patients will be expected to pay all relevant fees after
     each treatment they have received.

     One of the dentists has suggested offering patients a dental plan. The dental plan
     would cover the cost of all treatments for a patient in return for a flat monthly fee.
     This flat monthly fee would be fixed for 1 year and subject to annual increases
     thereafter.

     (i)     Explain why this dental plan may have been proposed.                             [2]

     (ii)    Describe how the assumptions for pricing the dental plan could be set.           [5]

     (iii)   Describe the risks to the dental practice of offering this plan.               [3]
                                                                                     [Total 10]


7    A successful music artist wants to sell the rights to future profits from their back
     catalogue of music (i.e. all music the artist has released to date).

     A financial company is considering whether to enter into a transaction with the artist
     to buy the rights of the artist’s back catalogue.

     (i)     Discuss the factors the financial company should consider in structuring the
             transaction.                                                                 [4]

     (ii)    Describe the risks, to each of the following key stakeholders, of such an
             arrangement:

                the artist
                the financial company.
                                                                                            [6]
                                                                                     [Total 10]

8    A developed country has a healthcare service that is free at the point of delivery for all
     citizens and is paid for out of general government revenue (and/or borrowing).

     The new government has proposed that the healthcare system should change to an
     insurance-based approach, where the citizen buys private health insurance from an
     insurance company.

     (i)     Suggest possible reasons why the new government has made this proposal. [4]

     (ii)    Discuss possible advantages and disadvantages of an insurance-based
             approach from the perspective of:

             (a)     the new government.

             (b)     insurance companies.

             (c)     individual citizens.
                                                                                             [7]
                                                                                      [Total 11]


9    An airline business transports cargo around the world.

     (i)     List the main risks associated with the transportation of cargo that the airline is
             taking on.                                                                      [3]

     Currently the airline does not insure any of the risks identified in part (i).

     (ii)    Describe how the airline could fund any significant losses arising from these
             risks.                                                                       [4]

     Following a year where the airline has had more losses than expected, the airline has
     decided to get quotations from general insurance companies for insurance cover for
     the transportation of cargo.

     (iii)   Set out how a general insurance company would determine the premium for
             providing the cover.                                                    [5]
                                                                              [Total 12]


10   A life insurance company has £100 billion of immediate annuity liabilities on its
     balance sheet.

     (i)     Describe why this company would choose to invest in both domestic and
             overseas assets.                                                                [6]

     (ii)    Discuss how the company’s assets may compare to the assets held by a life
             insurance company with £1 billion of immediate annuity liabilities.        [7]
                                                                                 [Total 13]

11   An insurance company sells motor insurance to a wide range of customers. The
     company has decided to focus on insuring electric vehicles only and will no longer
     offer insurance for non-electric vehicles.

     (i)     Suggest reasons why the insurance company may have taken this decision. [3]

     (ii)    Explain why the insurance company should monitor the experience of this
             electric only business.                                                 [4]

     (iii)   Discuss how the results of the monitoring could be used.                     [4]

     (iv)    Suggest, with reasons, measures that could be put in place by the company to
             prevent fraudulent claims on this electric only business.                   [4]
