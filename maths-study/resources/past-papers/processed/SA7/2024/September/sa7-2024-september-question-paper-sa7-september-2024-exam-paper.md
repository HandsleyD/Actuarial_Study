1    A large developed economy experienced strong economic growth and strong stock
     market performance following the application of highly accommodative monetary and
     fiscal policies during a pandemic that lasted 2 years. During this time, many citizens
     were able to materially add to their savings and fiscal deficits were high.

     Subsequently the economy experienced sustained high rates of inflation and
     monetary policy was changed to be more restrictive, but fiscal policy continued
     to be maintained at a significant accommodative stance, with continued high fiscal
     deficits.

     (i)      Discuss how this may impact the following in both the short and medium

              (a)    economic growth                                                      [6]

              (f)    economic inequality.                                                 [4]

     Some economic commentators have suggested that the current fiscal policy may lead
     to a sovereign debt crisis in the economy.

     (ii)     Discuss why they may have this view.                                      [7]

2    A large financial institution has material exposure to the following three asset classes:

           Local government loans: These are loans made to local government bodies.
            Some of the loans have repayments linked to the income generated from specific
            projects. Loan maturities are between 10 and 15 years.
           Commercial real estate loans: These are loans made to companies to purchase
            or construct property for business use. Loan repayments increase annually with
            inflation, and borrowers tend to meet loan payments from the property rental
            income or from other business revenue if they are an owner occupier. Loan
            maturities are between 15 and 50 years.
           Professional landlord loans: These are loans made to professional landlords who
            provide housing for international students. Loan maturities are between 15 and
            30 years.

     (i)       Discuss the key risks for investors of each of these asset classes.          [9]

     The following recent events are expected to influence the investment environment:

           Global pandemic: A widespread global pandemic that led to increases in
            unemployment and significantly reduced travel between countries.
           Equal pay legislation: The government recently implemented an equal pay policy
            for civil servants that will impact employees of local governments. Under this
            policy, employees who were deemed underpaid are entitled to up to 5 years’ worth
            of back-pay as compensation. The definition of affected employees will be
            published in the next 3 months, and compensation payments must be made within
            a year.
           Rent freezes: All commercial and residential rents have been frozen for 2 years.

     (ii)      Explain how the events outlined above will impact each of these asset classes.
                                                                                         [15]

     (iii)     Briefly outline the measures the institution could take to manage the risks
               associated with exposure to these asset classes.                             [5]
                                                                                     [Total 29]


3    You are an investment manager in a small boutique asset management firm. The
     senior management team have not previously studied investment psychology.

     During an investment strategy meeting, following comments that you made, the
     senior management team asked you to describe the importance of ‘Nous’ and why
     having a proportionate degree of humility is essential in making profitable investment
     decisions.

     (i)       Explain the importance of Nous when making investment decisions.             [7]

     (ii)      Explain why a proportionate degree of humility is necessary for an investment
               manager.                                                                    [7]

4    Corporate pension funds in a developing nation pay a pre-defined benefit where
     annual payments at retirement are based on an individual’s final salary and the
     number of years contributing membership of the pension fund. Recently introduced
     regulations require corporate pension liabilities to be hedged with appropriate assets.

     A bank has created a vehicle called Bulk Pension Fund (BPF) to transfer all pension
     assets and liabilities from a company’s balance sheet. The assets and liabilities of the
     pension fund are transferred to a special purpose entity that is backed by the bank’s
     capital, with the assets and liabilities managed by the bank.

     (i)      Suggest why companies may want to transfer the entirety of their pension
              funds.                                                                   [4]

     (ii)     Outline the potential impact to a bank of completing several large BPF
              transactions.                                                                [6]

     A senior executive at the bank has suggested that large BPF transactions should be
     reinsured in overseas jurisdictions that have a more favourable regulatory
     environment.

     (iii)    Outline the advantages to the bank of reinsuring BPFs.                       [5]

     (iv)     Outline considerations for the bank of using overseas reinsurers for the BPF.
                                                                                          [4]

     The financial services regulator has expressed a concern about the regulatory
     requirements for overseas reinsurers being weaker than for domestic reinsurers.

     (v)      Explain why the regulator could be concerned about overseas reinsurance
              arrangements for BPFs.                                                    [5]
