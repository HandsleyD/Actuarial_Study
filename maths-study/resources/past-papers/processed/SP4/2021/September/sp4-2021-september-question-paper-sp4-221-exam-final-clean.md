5     A company sponsors two defined benefit pension schemes – ‘Scheme A – Main
      Scheme’ and ‘Scheme X – Executive Scheme’.

      Your firm is the actuarial advisor to the scheme managers of Scheme A. The company
      has now appointed your firm as actuarial advisor to Scheme X, replacing the previous
      actuarial advisor.

      The actuarial valuation of both schemes with an effective date of 31 January 2021 is
      due.

      (i)     Describe the information you will need to request from the previous actuarial
              advisor, or the managers, in order to perform the actuarial valuation of
              Scheme X.                                                                   [9]

      As at 31 January 2020, the schemes had the following funding positions on their
      funding basis:

                Scheme (figures in millions)      Scheme A       Scheme X
                Assets                              2,400            500
                Liabilities                         2,000          1,100
                Surplus/deficit                      400            (600)

      You have asked an actuarial student at your firm to perform the actuarial valuation for
      both schemes at 31 January 2021. They present to you the results as follows:

               Scheme (figures in millions)      Scheme A        Scheme X
               Assets                              2,600             600
               Liabilities                         2,200           2,600
               Surplus/deficit                       400          (2,000)

      The funding basis adopted for Scheme X was agreed by the managers to be in line
      with that adopted for Scheme A, but updated for market conditions as at 31 January
      2021.

      (ii)    Discuss possible reasons for the change in funding position for Scheme X.
              Your answer should take consideration of the change in the funding position
              for Scheme A.                                                             [10]

      A vehicle manufacturing company is in financial distress. It sponsors a large defined

      (ii)    Discuss the implications of the managers accepting this request.             [8]

              place by the scheme’s managers to feel more comfortable in accepting this
              request.                                                                    [8]

      reduction contributions. The company finance director has suggested that the payment
      of transfer values to members leaving the scheme is also suspended during this period.
