     adjusted each year in line with two additional factors: price inflation and overall
     company performance. The proposed base values for the first year, dependent on
     employee performance rating, are as follows:

                                                   Cash lump sum
                     Performance rating
                                                         ($)
                     Poor                                 0
                     Satisfactory                      100
                     Good                              400
                     Excellent                       1,000

     profits made by the travel agency each year, as follows:

                                                      Cash lump sum
                   Company profits                     adjustment
                                                           (%)
                   Significantly below target                0
                   Below target                             50
                   On target                               100
                   Above target                            120
                   Significantly above target              150

     (ii)     Discuss the key factors the company should consider when deciding on an
