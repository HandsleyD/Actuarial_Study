1     A company sponsors a Defined Contribution (DC) pension scheme for its employees
      and is considering introducing a Defined Benefit (DB) underpin as part of the benefit
      package.

      (i)     Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of a DB underpin from the
              viewpoint of:

              (a)    the employees.

              (b)    the company.
                                                                                           [8]

      (ii)    List further design changes the company could make to enhance the members’
              benefit package in relation to the DC pension scheme.                    [3]
                                                                                [Total 11]


2     A government of a developing country is considering introducing a state sponsored
      pension for its citizens.

      (i)     Explain the factors the government may consider in deciding how to fund the
              benefit.                                                                  [8]

      The government has proposed that the state pension will be a fixed amount payable to
      citizens from their 65th birthday provided they have been in employment for at least
      40 years.

      (ii)    Discuss the suitability of the government’s proposal from the perspective of
              the citizens of the country.                                                 [6]
                                                                                   [Total 14]




SP4 S2024–2
3     A company sponsors a DB pension scheme (the Scheme) that is open to future benefit
      accrual. The benefits provided by the Scheme are linked to inflation. The country in
      which the company operates is currently experiencing high levels of inflation.

      (i)     Describe the key risks to the Scheme arising from high inflation, in relation to:

              (a)    investment.

              (b)    funding.

              (c)    sponsor covenant.
                                                                                            [9]

      (ii)    Outline how the scheme managers might manage the risks identified in
              part (i).                                                                     [5]

      The company has informed the scheme managers that it expects to see a fall in profits
      over the next year due to the high levels of inflation.

      (iii)   Describe what actions the scheme managers should take in the short term to
              manage any potential impact on the Scheme.                                 [4]

      The next funding valuation of the Scheme is not due for another 2 years. The scheme
      managers have asked their actuary to provide them with an updated funding position
      allowing for the latest expectations of inflation.

      (iv)    Describe how the actuary might set the inflation assumption.                 [6]
                                                                                    [Total 24]


4     An actuary, who has been advising a DB pension scheme for over 15 years, is about
      to undertake the next funding valuation for the scheme.

      (i)     Describe the role of the actuary in a funding valuation.                      [6]

      (ii)    Give examples of how the actuary would comply with professional
              requirements during the valuation process.                                    [4]

      (iii)   List the key information that the actuary will require to undertake the
              valuation.                                                                    [9]
                                                                                     [Total 19]

5     A DB pension scheme (the Scheme) has a funding strategy in place such that if the
      funding level increases, some of the risk is removed from the investment strategy.
      This is achieved by reducing the level of return-seeking assets and increasing the level
      of matching assets.

      (i)     Outline the process the scheme managers might use to implement the strategy,
              including how it might be monitored.                                      [7]

      The scheme valuation has just taken place, which shows that the funding position has
      increased and the Scheme is now in surplus.

      (ii)    Outline possible ways the surplus could be used.                              [4]

      The Scheme’s investment advisers have identified a new investment opportunity that
      is a direct investment in an infrastructure project in the renewable energy sector.

      (iii)   Set out the key considerations for the scheme managers in deciding if this is
              an appropriate investment for the scheme.                                     [6]
                                                                                  [Total 17]


6     A manufacturing company sponsors a large DB pension scheme (the Scheme). The
      funding level of the Scheme has recently been improving, so the company has
      approached an insurance company to obtain a buy-out quotation for securing all of the
      Scheme’s liabilities.

      (i)     Describe the key assumptions the insurance company will make to place a
              value on the Scheme’s liabilities.                                      [9]

      (ii)    Discuss how the assumptions identified in part (i) are likely to differ to those
              used in the Scheme’s funding valuation.                                        [4]

      (iii)   Discuss briefly why the company may want to approach more than one
              insurance company for a buy-out quotation.                             [2]
                                                                              [Total 15]
