2    A life insurance company issues a 25-year term assurance policy to a life aged 32
     exact. The worksheet ‘Q2 Base’ gives details of the premiums charged and benefits
     payable, as well as the reserving basis used.

     (i)       Calculate:

               (a)     The gross premium prospective reserve at the end of each policy year,
                       i.e. tVpro.

               (b)     The gross premium retrospective reserve at the end of each policy year,
                       i.e. tVretro.
                                                                                          [23]

     (ii)      Discuss briefly why the prospective and retrospective reserves are not equal
               for this policy.                                                             [4]

4    A life insurance company issues a 4-year joint life term assurance to two lives, a man
     currently aged 44 exact and a woman currently aged 46 exact. The premiums charged
     and the benefits payable under the policy are set out in the worksheet ‘Q4 Base’,
     together with the profit test basis used by the company to price the product.

     (i)     Calculate, for each month of the policy:

             (a)    the probability (per policy in force at outset) that both policyholders
                    survive to the start of the month.

             (b)    the probability (per policy in force at the start of the month) that a
                    death benefit is paid in that month.
                                                                                               [7]

     (ii)    Calculate the gross annual premium.                                              [28]

     The company is proposing to offer another version of this policy where the death
     benefit is payable at the end of the year, rather than at the end of the month, of the
     first death.

     (iii)   Discuss, without performing any calculations, how the gross premium would
             change under this proposal.                                              [5]
