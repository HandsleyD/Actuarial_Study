2    An investor buys 500 shares in Company A at a total price of £13,886. The shares are
     sold ex-dividend and the investor assumes that they will hold the shares in perpetuity.

     The company pays dividends every 6 months, and the latest dividend of £1.10 per
     share was paid 6 months ago. The investor expects dividends to increase at a rate
     of 5.5% p.a., with increases applied half-yearly. Future inflation is expected to be
     3.5% p.a.

     Calculate, showing all working, the expected annual effective real yield.              [5]

4    An investor has just purchased an office block for £4.8 million. The investor will
     spend a further £700,000 on refurbishments in 3 months’ time.

     Company XYZ has agreed to rent the office block 10 months from now. The contract
     states that Company XYZ will rent the office block from the investor for 20 years and
     will then purchase the property at the end of the 20-year rental period for £5.7 million.

     Rent will be paid monthly in advance and will be increased every year at the rate of
     3.5% p.a. compound. The initial rent is set at £800,000 p.a. with the first rental
     payment due immediately on the date of occupation.

     Use an effective rate of interest of 8% p.a. and assume that the investor does not pay
     tax.

     (i)     Calculate, showing all working and using suitable annuity factors, the net
             present value of the project valued at the time of purchase.                   [9]

     (ii)    Suggest how your answer to part (i) would change if the rent were to be paid
             continuously. You are not required to do any calculations.                  [2]
                                                                                 [Total 11]


5    An insurance company issues a 3-year increasing temporary annuity to a life aged
     65 exact.

     The annuity payments, to be made annually in arrears, in years 1, 2 and 3 are £9,000,
     £11,000 and £15,000, respectively.

     The policyholder purchases the annuity with a single premium of £31,000.

     The present value of the profit, X, earned by the company from the contract is a
     random variable given by:

                   X  PV of premium income  PV of benefit outgo

     (i)     Construct the distribution function of the random variable X (i.e. find all
             possible values of the random variable, together with the probability that the
             random variable takes each value).                                            [4]

     (ii)    Calculate, showing all working, the mean and variance of the present value of
             the profit earned by the company.                                          [5]

     (iii)   Comment on your answers to part (ii).                                          [2]

     Mortality      ELT15 (Females)
     Interest       5.5% p.a. effective

7    A 30-year loan of $500,000 is repaid by quarterly instalments in arrears. The
     instalments are level over each year and increase by $100 at the start of each
     following year. The effective rate of interest applicable to the loan is 7% p.a.
     compounded quarterly.

     (i)       Calculate, showing all working and using appropriate annuity factors, the
               initial quarterly instalment.                                                  [8]

     (ii)      Calculate, showing all working and using appropriate annuity factors, the
               capital and interest elements of the third instalment.                         [4]

     (iii)     Calculate, showing all working and using appropriate annuity factors, the
               capital outstanding before the payment of the penultimate (i.e. the second to
               last) quarterly instalment.                                                   [2]

     (iv)      (a)    Write down a formula for the total interest paid over the term of the
                      loan.

               (b)    Calculate the total interest paid over the term of the loan using your

8    A company has liabilities of $Z falling due at the end of 2 years and $3,200 at the end
     of 4 years. The current annual effective interest rate is 7% p.a. The company plans to
     immunise the interest rate risk by investing in three zero-coupon bonds as follows:

           a 2-year zero-coupon bond with a nominal value of $298.60
           a 3-year zero-coupon bond with a nominal value of $2,571.00
           a 4-year zero-coupon bond with a nominal value of $1,824.51.

     (i)       Calculate, showing all working, the amount $Z such that the present value of
               the assets is equal to the present value of the liabilities.               [4]

     (ii)      Determine numerically, showing all working, if the portfolio is immunised
               according to Redington’s conditions.                                       [9]
