2    A life insurance company issues a joint life policy to a male life aged 30 exact and a
     female life aged 25 exact that provides the following benefits:

           A joint life term assurance. The benefit is paid at the end of the year of the first
            death, provided death occurs before the male life’s 65th birthday.
           A joint life deferred annuity. When in payment the annuity has a 5-year guarantee
            period. Thereafter the full amount is payable while both lives are alive and a
            reduced amount of 50% while only one life is alive. The annuity is payable
            annually in advance. The first payment takes place on the male life’s 65th birthday
            provided both lives are alive.

     If the term assurance benefit is paid, then the policy terminates and no further benefits
     are payable.

     The basis used by the life insurance company is set out in the worksheet ‘Q2 Base’.

     (i)       Calculate the expected value of the joint life term assurance at the date of
               issue.                                                                       [10]

     (ii)      Calculate the expected value of the joint life deferred annuity at the date of
               issue.                                                                        [24]

     (iii)     Explain, without performing any calculations, how your final numerical
               answers to parts (i) and (ii) would change if the female life had been aged 35.
                                                                                             [6]
                                                                                      [Total 40]

3    A life insurance company issues a special unit-linked 20 year endowment assurance
     policy to a life aged 40 exact. Premiums increase each year and are payable annually
     in advance.

     If the policyholder dies during the term of the policy, the death benefit is equal to the
     year-end bid value of units (after the deduction of the management charge) multiplied
     by 2. The benefit is payable at the end of the year of death. The maturity benefit is
     equal to the bid value of the units (after the deduction of the management charge).

     Full policy conditions and the basis used to profit test the policy are given in the
     ‘Q3 Base’ worksheet.

     (i)     Calculate the profit vector for this policy.                                   [23]

     The company now sets up reserves to zeroise all future negative cashflows.

     (ii)    (a)    Calculate the reserves required at the end of each policy year.

             (b)    Calculate the revised profit vector for the policy.
                                                                                             [7]

     The company uses a risk discount rate of 8% p.a. to calculate the present value of
     profits.

     (iii)   Calculate the present value of profits both before and after zeroisation.       [7]

     The company has reviewed the non-unit fund growth rate assumption and it now
     intends to use a fixed growth rate of 8% p.a.

     (iv)    Discuss how your answer to part (iii) would change in light of this new
             assumption. Do not perform any further calculations.                         [3]
