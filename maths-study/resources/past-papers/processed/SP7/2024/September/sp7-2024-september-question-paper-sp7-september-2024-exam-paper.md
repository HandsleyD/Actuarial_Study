2     Company J is a general insurance company writing a range of lines of business in
      Country L and overseas. Recently Country L was impacted by a series of typhoons
      resulting in large catastrophe losses for Company J, some of which were recovered
      from its reinsurance programme.

      Company J’s management have decided to set up an offshore reinsurance subsidiary
      to write reinsurance business.

      (i)     Suggest possible reasons why Company J may want to set up an offshore
              reinsurance subsidiary.                                                        [3]

      Company J is reviewing the impact of setting up the offshore reinsurance subsidiary
      on its capital requirements.

      (ii)    Explain, for each risk type, how owning the reinsurance subsidiary may
              impact the capital requirement of Company J.                                   [6]

      Company J is considering using a stochastic capital model to estimate the capital
      requirement of the reinsurance subsidiary.

      (iii)   Suggest possible reasons why Company J is considering a stochastic model for
              this purpose.                                                             [4]

6     Company F is a general insurance company that operates through a number of
      branches located in different countries. Company F underwrites business in the
      currency of each country it operates in and claims are also settled in the local
      currency. Company F underwrites business in 15 different currencies globally.

      The company maintains cash balances and investments in all 15 currencies. However,
      for the purpose of Incurred But Not Reported (IBNR) estimation, the company uses
      only three currencies: US dollars (USD), Euros and British pounds.

      (i)       Suggest possible reasons why the company is reserving in only these three
                currencies.                                                               [3]

      (ii)      Discuss the advantages and disadvantages to Company F of estimating IBNR
                in only three currencies.                                              [4]

      (iii)     Set out how Company F can calculate the movement in IBNR between two
                valuation dates.                                                     [3]

      Currently Company F purchases all reinsurance in the local currency of the country in
      which the business is being sold, with any excesses and limits agreed in the local
      currency only. Company F holds the assets backing its liabilities in different
      currencies.

      The group Chief Underwriting Officer (CUO) has proposed the following:

            All reinsurance to be purchased at the group level with all policy excesses and
             limits quoted in USD.
            All assets held to match its liabilities to be held in USD.

      (iv)      Comment on the group CUO’s proposal.                                         [5]
