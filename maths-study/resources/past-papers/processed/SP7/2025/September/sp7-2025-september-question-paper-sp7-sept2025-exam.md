2     (i)     Outline the possible risks associated with doubling of premiums in one
              calendar year for a general insurance company.                                [4]

      A general insurance company experienced a doubling of its written premium
      in calendar year 2024. However, the reserves held on the balance sheet as at
      31 December 2024 remained unchanged, i.e. the same amount of reserves as at
      31 December 2023. The regulator is concerned about the sufficiency of the reserves
      given the increased volume of business written.

      (ii)    Explain why the regulator’s concern may not be valid.                        [3]

      (iii)   Suggest possible ratios that the regulator could calculate using the accounting
              items in the insurance company’s financial statements to determine if the
              company’s reserves are sufficient.                                            [4]

3     (i)     List four natural catastrophe events that could impact a general insurance
              company writing commercial property insurance.                               [2]

      Company A is a general insurance company writing commercial property insurance.
      In recent years Company A has experienced an increase in natural catastrophe events
      causing large losses to a few single risks.

      (ii)    Suggest possible actions Company A could take to reduce its exposure to
              natural catastrophe events impacting single risks.                           [3]

      Company A currently has a risk excess of loss reinsurance arrangement, $20 million
      xs $10 million, covering individual losses from single risks, including losses caused
      by natural catastrophe perils. Company A also has a catastrophe excess of loss
      arrangement, $100 million xs $200 million, covering the aggregation of losses from a
      natural catastrophe event, net of recoveries from the risk excess of loss reinsurance
      arrangement.

      Company A experienced losses from a recent natural catastrophe event and has
      provided the following loss information from this event:

      Individual risk losses greater than $5 million:

                                    Risk      Loss ($)
                                     A       20,000,000
                                     B       50,000,000
                                     C        7,500,000

      Accumulation of losses from other risks: $240,000,000.

      (iii)   Calculate, showing all workings and stating any assumption made, the
              recoveries from this event from:

              (a)    the risk excess of loss arrangement.

              (b)    the catastrophe excess of loss arrangement.
                                                                                           [7]

      The reinsurance company has informed Company A that they will be removing the
      natural catastrophe coverage from the risk excess of loss reinsurance arrangement at
      the next renewal.

      (iv)    Outline the impact on the risks modelled in Company A’s capital model from
              the change in reinsurance coverage.                                      [3]
                                                                                [Total 15]

5     Company R is a general insurance company writing a portfolio of Employers’
      Liability (EL) insurance. Large claims are settled either upfront as a lump sum or in
      the form of payments occurring at regular intervals over several years.

      (i)       Discuss the advantages and disadvantages for Company R of settling claims
                as a lump sum versus at regular intervals.                                [4]

      Due to a recent period of high inflation, the actuarial department of Company R has
      decided to allow for the impact of inflation explicitly in the Incurred But Not
      Reported (IBNR) estimation using a superimposed inflation rate. Previously the
      impact of inflation was allowed for implicitly in the actuarial assumptions.

      (ii)      Describe, with reasons, the likely components of the superimposed inflation
                rate for the EL business for Company R.                                     [4]

      (iii)     Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of allowing for the impact of
                inflation explicitly instead of implicitly in the IBNR estimation.             [4]

      (iv)      Describe how to model the impact of inflation on the various risks in
                Company R’s capital model.                                                     [6]
                                                                                        [Total 18]
