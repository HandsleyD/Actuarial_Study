3    A few years ago, an insurance company, Company Q, purchased an excess of loss
     multi-year contract providing 3 years of reinsurance coverage for its property
     insurance book.

     The multi-year contract Company Q purchased has the following terms:

           Excess of loss contract covering losses occurring between 1 January 2022 and
            31 December 2024 inclusive.
           Layer limit $10 million.
           Layer attachment point $20 million.
           Annual reinstatement terms: one reinstatement payable at 100% additional
            premium pro-rata to amount reinstated.
           Rate on line: 55% payable for each coverage calendar year.
           Profit commission payable at the end of the contract equal to 100% of the balance
            in the experience account, if positive.
           Experience account formula:

            60% × Annual premiums + 100% Reinstatement premiums − Layer recoveries

     (i)       Suggest possible reasons why Company Q purchased this contract instead of a
               single year reinsurance cover.                                          [3]

     The following loss information has been provided by Company Q showing all ground
     up property losses above $10 million for each coverage year:

                 Coverage year     Date                   Ground up loss ($)
                     2022          28 February 2022          24,000,000
                     2022          6 April 2022              15,000,000
                     2022          17 November 2022          21,000,000
                     2023          9 August 2023             45,000,000
                     2024          13 January 2024           12,000,000

     (ii)      Calculate, showing all workings and stating any assumptions made, the profit
               commission payable to Company Q at the end of the 3 years.               [10]
