     The Fama–French three-factor model may be presented by the following expression:

                       R = Rf + ß1(Rm – Rf) + ß2(SMB) + ß3(HML) + α

     where:

           R is the expected total return of a stock or portfolio.
           Rf is the risk-free rate of return.
           Rm is the total market portfolio return.
           ß1,2,3 are factor coefficients.
           α is risk.
           SMB is Small Minus Big and measures the excess returns of small market
            capitalisation stocks over large market capitalisation stocks.
           HML is High Minus Low and measures the excess returns of stocks with high
            book/price values compared with stocks with low book/price values.
