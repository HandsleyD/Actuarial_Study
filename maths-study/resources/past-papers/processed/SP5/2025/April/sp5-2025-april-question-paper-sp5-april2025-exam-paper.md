6    Country A’s economy has experienced a prolonged period of benign economic
     conditions (i.e. low inflation, low interest rates and moderate economic growth).
     Country A has an open economy, i.e. one in which there is significant trading with
     other countries.

     (i)      Describe the possible economic impact of a sharp and unexpected increase in
              inflation in Country A.                                                   [7]

     A global macro hedge fund manager anticipates a sharp increase in global inflation
     within the next 12 months. This view is not widely held by other market participants.
     The hedge fund manager wishes to position their portfolio to take advantage of this.
     The fund’s performance target is to outperform cash by 4% p.a.

     (ii)     Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of the hedge fund manager
              investing in the following asset classes:

              (a)    cash

              (b)    listed global equities

              (c)    inflation-protected government securities

              (d)    infrastructure, via pooled funds.
                                                                                             [8]

     In actual fact, the inflation rate did not rise as the fund manager anticipated, central
     banks tightened money supply and increased interest rates which kept inflation low.

     (iii)    Discuss the impact of these actions on the economy of Country A.               [4]

     (iv)     State with reasons which of the strategies in part (ii) is likely to have been
              most successful in the scenario described in part (iii).                        [2]
