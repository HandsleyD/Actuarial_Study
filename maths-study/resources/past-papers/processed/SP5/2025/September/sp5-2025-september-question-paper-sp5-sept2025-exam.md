4     The government of Country A wants to ensure that its population saves for their
      retirement and is introducing a compulsory Defined Contribution (DC) pension
      initiative. Under the initiative everyone who is in employment will be required to
      contribute a fixed percentage of their salary into a DC pension scheme offered by
      financial services companies. The government of Country A is also introducing
      legislation to ensure that the financial service companies included in the initiative are
      safe for employees to invest in.

      (i)     Suggest the possible general principles that the legislation should promote. [3]

      (ii)    Describe how the financial companies should be structured.                     [3]

      (iii)   Describe the key elements that the proposed legislation should contain in
              relation to their dealings with customers.                                     [3]

      After the compulsory DC pension scheme was launched, a number of financial
      services companies offered pension schemes for those who are required to invest. All
      of the pension schemes are tax free.

      Employees will be provided with detailed information about the financial services
      companies and their pension schemes to help them choose which company and which
      funds in those companies to use.

      (iv)    Discuss what information the potential investors should look for before
              deciding where to invest their contributions.                               [5]
