## Key Ideas

- Demand: quantity demanded falls as price rises, other things equal.
- Supply: quantity supplied rises as price rises, other things equal.
- Equilibrium: price and quantity where demand equals supply.
- Shifts vs movements: price changes move along a curve; other factor changes shift the curve.
- Main demand shifters: income, tastes, prices of substitutes/complements, expectations, population.
- Main supply shifters: input costs, technology, taxes/subsidies, expectations, number of sellers.
- Disequilibrium: excess demand at prices below equilibrium; excess supply above equilibrium.
- Elasticity is key for understanding how strongly quantity responds to price or income changes.
