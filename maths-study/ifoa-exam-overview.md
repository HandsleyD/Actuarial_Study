# IFoA Exam Overview (Planned Subjects)

> Note: Use this as a quick planning guide. Confirm current syllabus wording from IFoA before each sitting.

## CM1 — Actuarial Mathematics 1
- Interest theory, annuities, loans, bonds, and life contingencies.
- Core life insurance and pension-style cashflow mathematics.

## CM2 — Actuarial Mathematics 2
- Financial economics, stochastic investment models, and derivatives.
- Portfolio theory, option pricing, and risk/return modelling.

## CS1 — Actuarial Statistics 1
- Probability and statistical foundations.
- Inference, regression, and introductory data analysis.

## CS2 — Actuarial Statistics 2
- Advanced statistics for actuarial modelling.
- Survival models, risk models, time series, simulation, and GLM-style methods.

## CB1 — Business Finance
- Accounting, corporate finance basics, financial statements, and investment appraisal.

## CB2 — Business Economics
- Microeconomics and macroeconomics applied to business and financial contexts.

## CB3 — Business Management
- Business and management skills, communication, and decision-making in practice.

## CP1 — Actuarial Practice
- End-to-end actuarial problem solving across practice areas.
- Integrates technical judgment, professionalism, and recommendations.

## CP2 — Modelling Practice
- Spreadsheet/data modelling, testing, validation, and model communication.

## CP3 — Communication Practice
- Communicating technical actuarial content clearly to non-technical audiences.

## SP2 — Life Insurance
- Specialist life insurance product design, valuation, risk, and management.

## SP4 — Pensions and Other Benefits
- Specialist pensions design, funding, valuation, regulation, and advice.

## SA4 — Pensions and Other Benefits Advanced
- Advanced pensions strategy, governance, risk, and complex advisory work.
